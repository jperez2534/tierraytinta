About Us

Slow crafted in Miami, Florida

This business was started by two young women with a dream to bring beauty into this world and share it with others.

Tierra y Tinta is a custom art jewelry studio focused on turning real orchids into one-of-a-kind earrings and necklaces. Every piece starts with carefully selected orchids, which we preserve and transform by hand into wearable art.

We believe in slow crafting: small batches, attention to detail, and a personal touch. Our inventory stays intentionally limited so that each order is treated with the same care. We’re based in Miami, Florida, where the light and the pace inspire our process.

Our values

  • Real orchids — We use actual orchids in our pieces, not imitations.
  • Handcrafted — Each item is made by hand, with care and time.
  • Personal — We think of every customer as a beloved part of our story.
